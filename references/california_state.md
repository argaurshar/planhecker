# California state codes — selected references

Illustrative starter content. Verify each value against the current code edition before treating any finding as authoritative.

## California Residential Code (CRC) — common items

### Stairs (CRC R311.7)
- Minimum stair width: 36 in (914 mm) clear, measured above handrail height
- Maximum riser height: 7-3/4 in (197 mm) (CRC R311.7.5.1)
- Minimum tread depth: 10 in (254 mm) (CRC R311.7.5.2)
- Minimum headroom above stair walking surface: 6 ft 8 in (2032 mm) (CRC R311.7.2)
- Handrails required on at least one side for stairs with 4+ risers (CRC R311.7.8)

### Egress windows (CRC R310.2.1)
- Every sleeping room and basement habitable space must have an emergency escape and rescue opening (EERO)
- Minimum net clear opening: 5.7 sq ft (5.0 sq ft for grade-floor)
- Minimum net clear opening height: 24 in
- Minimum net clear opening width: 20 in
- Maximum sill height above floor: 44 in (CRC R310.2.2)

### Smoke and CO alarms (CRC R314, R315)
- Smoke alarms in every sleeping room, immediately outside each separate sleeping area, and on every story
- Carbon monoxide alarms outside each separate sleeping area, on every habitable level, and in attached garages
- Required hard-wired with battery backup; interconnected on new construction

### Guards and handrails (CRC R312)
- Guards required on open sides of walking surfaces more than 30 in above grade
- Minimum guard height: 36 in (42 in for decks per some local amendments)
- Openings: must reject a 4 in sphere (4-3/8 in for guards on stair sides)

## California Building Code (CBC) — common items

### Means of egress (CBC §1011)
- Minimum egress door clear width: 32 in
- Minimum egress door height: 80 in
- Egress doors must swing in direction of travel for occupant load > 50

### Accessibility (CBC Chapter 11A — single-family / 11B — non-residential)
- Multifamily covered occupancy: CBC 11A "covered multifamily dwellings" rules apply
- Single-family is generally exempt from accessibility unless owner-financed by federal funds; verify

## Title 24, Part 6 (Building Energy Efficiency Standards)
- All new residential construction must comply with the current Title 24 cycle (2022 or 2025 — verify vesting)
- Mandatory measures (envelope, windows, mechanical, lighting, water heating) plus prescriptive OR performance compliance path
- HERS verification required for several measures (duct sealing, refrigerant charge, etc.)
- All-electric or mixed-fuel: many jurisdictions have local "reach codes" requiring electrification — verify per project

## California ADU state law (Government Code §65852.2)

### Detached new-construction ADU
- AB 68 / AB 881 / SB 13 / AB 3182 (and successor amendments) preempt many local restrictions
- Maximum size: 1,200 sq ft (or higher per local rules) — but minimum 800 sq ft must be allowed even if local rules are more restrictive
- Setbacks: 4 ft side and rear from a detached ADU is the state-allowed minimum (local may be more permissive)
- Owner occupancy cannot be required (post-2020)
- Parking: cannot be required for ADUs within 1/2 mile of public transit, or in historic districts, or for one-bedroom-or-less units near transit (verify against current Gov. Code language)
- Permit shot-clock: 60 days for ministerial review (post-completion of application)

### SB 9 (Government Code §§65852.21, 66411.7)
- Allows up to 4 units on a single-family-zoned lot via a combination of urban lot split (66411.7) and 2-unit development (65852.21)
- Owner-occupancy affidavit required (3-year minimum)
- Maximum 4 ft setback for new units; ministerial review

## Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) — CBC Chapter 7A / CRC R337
- Required materials: ignition-resistant exterior wall covering, Class A roof, ember-resistant vents
- Applies in state-mapped Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones AND in many local hazard maps
- Verify lot's WUI status with the AHJ — some Bay Area cities apply CRC R337 to Moderate / High zones too
